# 🦇 Movie Spotlight: The Dark Knight

An exploration of Christopher Nolan's legendary superhero masterpiece, **The Dark Knight**.

---

## 📌 Synopsis
Batman, Lieutenant Jim Gordon, and District Attorney Harvey Dent form an alliance to dismantle organized crime in Gotham City. However, their efforts are upended by a chaotic criminal mastermind known only as the Joker.

> **Key Quote:** "You either die a hero or you live long enough to see yourself become the villain."

---

## 🍿 Movie Details
* **Director:** Christopher Nolan
* **Release Year:** 2008
* **Genres:** Superhero / Crime / Drama
* **Key Accolades:** 2 Academy Awards (including Best Supporting Actor for Heath Ledger)

---

## 🃏 Major Characters

| Character | Actor | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Bruce Wayne / Batman** | Christian Bale | Gotham's vigilante protector |
| **The Joker** | Heath Ledger | Agent of chaos testing Gotham's moral boundaries |
| **Harvey Dent** | Aaron Eckhart | Gotham's heroic D.A. who falls from grace |

---

## 🏆 Standout Sequence Highlights
1. **The Bank Heist:** A masterclass opening introducing the Joker's unpredictable logic.
2. **The Armored Truck Chase:** Featuring practical stunts and the Batpod flip.
3. **The Interrogation Scene:** High-intensity moral standoff between Batman and the Joker.
